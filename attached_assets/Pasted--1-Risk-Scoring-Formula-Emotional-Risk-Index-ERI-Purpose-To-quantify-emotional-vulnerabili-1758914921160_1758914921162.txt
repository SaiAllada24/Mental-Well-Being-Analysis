🔹 1. Risk Scoring Formula — Emotional Risk Index (ERI)
🎯 Purpose
To quantify emotional vulnerability across multiple dimensions and express it as a score (0–100), then bucket into Low / Medium / High.

🧮 Formula Structure
Let’s define weights for each signal based on impact:

Signal	Weight
Stress	0.25
Coping Struggles	0.20
Habit Change	0.15
Mood Swings	0.15
Work Interest Loss	0.10
Social Weakness	0.10
Days Indoors	0.05
🧠 Scoring Logic
Each signal is scored on a normalized scale (0–1):

Binary signals (Yes/No): Yes = 1, No = 0

Mood Swings: Low = 0, Medium = 0.5, High = 1

Days Indoors:

Go out every day = 0

1–14 = 0.25

15–30 = 0.5

31–60 = 0.75

🧮 Final Calculation
text
ERI = ∑(Signal Score × Weight)
ERI × 100 → Final Score (0–100)
🧭 Risk Buckets
ERI Score	Risk Level
0–33	Low
34–66	Medium
67–100	High
🔹 2. Adaptive Suggestions — Logic Tree
🎯 Purpose
To generate personalized “Immediate Action” and “Medium-Term Goal” based on:

Gender

Occupation

Days Indoors

Risk Level

🌳 Logic Tree Structure
Example Branch:
Gender: Female Occupation: Homemaker Days Indoors: >60 Risk Level: High

→ Immediate Action: “Schedule a virtual check-in with a friend or relative this week. Choose someone who listens without judgment.”

Medium-Term Goal: “Explore community-based support apps or local women’s wellness circles. Rebuild social rhythm gently.”

Another Branch:
Gender: Male Occupation: Corporate Days Indoors: 15–30 Risk Level: Medium

→ Immediate Action: “Block 30 minutes this week for uninterrupted reflection — no screens, no meetings.”

Medium-Term Goal: “Try journaling or guided meditation to process stress. Consider a peer support group for professionals.”

🧠 Suggestion Modules
Each suggestion is tagged by:

Theme: Social / Emotional / Routine / Cognitive

Format: Text / Link / App / Helpline

Disclosure Level: Summary / Expandable
